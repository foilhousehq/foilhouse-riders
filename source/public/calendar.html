BEFORE YOU GO

A few useful details.

Are all event dates confirmed?

FOILHOUSE HQ’s attendance at the 10 listed U.S. events is confirmed. Many exact event dates have not yet been supplied. Those listings show “Exact dates to be announced.” Confirm dates, launch access, lodging, and registration with the event organizer before booking.

How do I register or book lodging?

Registration and lodging links will be added when verified. Flatland Foil and Boil has a $300 event fee. Book rooms before they fill up, and contact FOILHOUSE HQ through its main website for current event information.

How do saved events work?

Tap the heart on an event to save it in this browser on this device. No account is required. Saved events do not sync between devices and may be cleared if you clear browser data.

Can I put this on my phone’s home screen?

Yes. Open this website in your phone’s browser. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the browser menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app, if offered). Availability depends on your browser. The hub needs an internet connection for current information.

Will this show team appearances and sponsors?

Confirmed athlete appearances, official event flyers, and approved sponsor information will be added when available. Event listings do not guarantee attendance by any individual athlete.
